Family Bot

Family Bot is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by one household. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

  • Data accessed: Google Calendar events and Google Tasks of the household's own account, only to create, list and delete items at the owner's request.
  • Storage: Data is processed on the household's own server. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Third parties: Message text may be sent to Google Gemini to understand requests.
  • Revoke access: Any time via Google Account - Third-party connections.
